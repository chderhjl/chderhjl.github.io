About

I am currently pursuing a PhD in Transportation Engineering at the School of Future Transportation, Chang'an University. My research centers on the temperature-related behaviour of steel bridges — in particular temperature-induced fatigue — and on developing deep-learning methods to make bridge monitoring data more reliable and informative.

Education

  • B.E. in Road, Bridge and River Crossing Engineering (Bridge Engineering) — Chang'an University, 2021–2025
  • B.E. in Artificial Intelligence — Chang'an University, 2021–2025

Research Interests

Temperature Fatigue of Steel Bridges

Investigating cyclic temperature-induced stresses and the fatigue performance of steel bridges under realistic service conditions.

Long-Term Temperature Field Modelling

Reconstructing full life-cycle temperature histories of steel bridges from limited field monitoring data.

Deep Learning for Bridge Monitoring

Applying deep learning and computer vision — including image restoration in adverse weather — to improve monitoring-data quality.
